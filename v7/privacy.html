1. Who We Are

Coperbyte LLC (“Coperbyte,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) designs, builds, and scales secure software products across web, mobile, and AI. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit Coperbyte.com or engage our services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Usage Data: pages viewed, links clicked, and interactions on our site.
  • Project Information: content you provide about your project needs.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve our website and services.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information only with:

5. Data Security

We employ administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—such as encryption, access controls, and monitoring—to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or to satisfy legal and compliance requirements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing communications. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@coperbyte.com.

8. International Transfers
